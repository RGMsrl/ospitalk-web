Data processing agreement

Annex to the Terms of service · art. 28 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") · Version 2026-10-30

In short. Your guests' data is yours: you are the data controller. RGM uses it only to run Ospitalk for you, protects it, doesn't use it to train artificial intelligence models, tells you if a provider changes and deletes it 90 days after check-out or at the end of the contract.

1. Parties and roles

This agreement is concluded between the host who uses Ospitalk ("controller") and RGM S.r.l., Via Giuseppe Garibaldi 23, 95010 Santa Venerina (CT), Italy, VAT number 05047840870 ("RGM" or "processor"). It is part of the Terms of service and is accepted together with them by replying ACCEPT on WhatsApp.

For the guests' personal data processed with Ospitalk the host is the data controller and RGM is the data processor under art. 28 GDPR. For the host's own data (sign-up, invoicing, support) RGM is instead an independent controller, as explained in the privacy notice.

2. Subject matter, nature and purpose of the processing

RGM processes guests' data for the sole purpose of providing Ospitalk to the controller, and in particular to:

  • receive the messages guests send to Ospitalk's WhatsApp number and link them to the property;
  • generate automatic answers based on the information provided by the controller, including by transcribing voice notes and reading the images sent;
  • forward to the controller the questions the assistant can't answer and deliver the controller's answers to the guest;
  • send the guest the access code during the stay registered by the controller;
  • keep the conversation history for the controller and ensure the security and proper functioning of the service.

Processing is carried out by electronic means and includes collection, recording, organisation, storage, consultation, automated processing (including processing with artificial intelligence systems), disclosure to sub-processors and erasure. It does not include decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects on guests.

3. Duration

This agreement lasts as long as the contract for the use of Ospitalk and, after it ends, until the data has been fully erased or returned as provided in article 11.

4. Categories of data subjects and of data

Data subjects: guests of the property and people who write to Ospitalk's WhatsApp number about one of the controller's properties (for example someone asking for information before booking); people who may be named in the content provided by the controller (for example helpers or cleaning staff).

Data:

  • WhatsApp phone number and profile name;
  • content of messages, voice notes and their transcripts, images and other files sent;
  • language of the conversation, date and time of messages, delivery status;
  • stay data registered by the controller (for example check-in and check-out dates, guest's name);
  • data of the controller's helpers included in the property content (name, phone number).

Ospitalk is not designed to process special categories of data (art. 9 GDPR) or data relating to criminal convictions (art. 10 GDPR). The controller must not ask guests for this data, or for identity documents, through Ospitalk. If a guest provides it spontaneously, RGM processes it only as part of the conversation and with the same security measures.

5. Controller's instructions

RGM processes the data only on the controller's documented instructions. The instructions consist of this agreement, the Terms of service and the settings the controller chooses in Ospitalk (for example the property content, the registered stays, the answers sent). The controller can give further instructions by writing to info@ospitalk.com; if they involve costs or changes to the service, RGM agrees them with the controller first.

RGM informs the controller immediately if it believes an instruction breaches the GDPR or other data protection rules. RGM may process the data without instructions only if required by Union or Italian law; in that case it informs the controller before the processing, unless the law prohibits it.

No training. RGM doesn't use guests' data for its own purposes, doesn't sell it and doesn't use it, or allow sub-processors to use it, to train or improve artificial intelligence models.

6. Controller's obligations

The controller guarantees that they have a legal basis for the processing, that they give guests the information required by arts. 13 and 14 GDPR (Ospitalk provides an information page for each property, whose link is sent to the guest in the first message, which the controller must check and complete) and that they don't enter into Ospitalk data they are not authorised to process.

7. Confidentiality

RGM ensures that the people authorised to process the data have committed themselves to confidentiality or are under a legal obligation of confidentiality, receive appropriate instructions and access the data only when needed to provide the service, give support at the controller's request or solve technical problems.

8. Security (art. 32 GDPR)

RGM takes technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk, including:

  • encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/TLS) between guest, WhatsApp, providers and servers;
  • servers located in the European Union, accessible only with personal authentication keys and with regular security updates;
  • logical separation of each controller's data and access by the controller through a personal link;
  • access codes sent only to guests with a stay in progress;
  • verification of the signature of messages received from the WhatsApp provider;
  • backup copies and restore procedures;
  • periodic review of the measures.

RGM updates the measures over time in line with technical developments and risks, without reducing the overall level of protection.

9. Sub-processors

The controller gives RGM a general authorisation to engage other processors ("sub-processors"). The up-to-date list, with service, data processed, location and safeguards for transfers, is published on the sub-processors page.

Before adding or replacing a sub-processor, RGM notifies the controller on WhatsApp at least 15 days in advance, except for justified urgent reasons of security or service continuity, in which case it notifies as soon as possible. Within this period the controller may object on reasonable data protection grounds by writing to info@ospitalk.com or replying on WhatsApp. If RGM cannot avoid the change, the controller may withdraw from the contract at no cost and obtain a refund of the unused part of the package.

RGM imposes on each sub-processor, by contract, data protection obligations substantially equivalent to those of this agreement, and remains liable to the controller for their performance.

10. Assistance to the controller

Data subjects' rights

Taking into account the nature of the processing, RGM assists the controller with appropriate measures in responding to requests from guests exercising the rights of arts. 15-22 GDPR (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection). If a guest contacts RGM directly, RGM forwards the request to the controller without delay and does not answer on the merits without instructions, other than telling the guest whom to contact.

Personal data breaches

RGM notifies the controller, on WhatsApp and by email, without undue delay and in any case within 48 hours of becoming aware of it, of any personal data breach affecting the controller's data. The notification describes, as far as known, the nature of the breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records, the likely consequences, the measures taken or proposed and a contact for further information; information not yet available is provided as soon as possible. RGM helps the controller assess whether to notify the supervisory authority within 72 hours and whether to communicate the breach to the data subjects (arts. 33 and 34 GDPR).

Other obligations

RGM gives the controller the information reasonably needed for any data protection impact assessments (art. 35 GDPR) and prior consultations with the supervisory authority (art. 36 GDPR) relating to Ospitalk.

11. Retention, erasure and return

  • During the contract: each guest's conversations and data are automatically deleted 90 days after the registered check-out; if no stay is registered, 90 days after the guest's last message. The controller can ask for earlier deletion.
  • At the end of the contract: the controller can ask within 30 days for a copy of the data in a readable format. After that RGM deletes all the controller's guest data, at the latest within 90 days of the end of the contract, including backups, which are overwritten on rotation within the same period.
  • Retention obligations imposed on RGM by law remain unaffected; in that case the data is kept only for the time and purpose required and protected as provided in this agreement.

On request, RGM confirms the erasure in writing.

12. Information and audits

RGM makes available to the controller the information needed to demonstrate compliance with this agreement and with art. 28 GDPR, answering the controller's questions in writing within 30 days. The controller, directly or through an auditor bound by confidentiality and not a competitor of RGM, may carry out an audit, including an inspection, with at least 30 days' notice, during working hours, without compromising the security of other customers and no more than once a year, except in case of data breaches or requests from an authority. Audits are normally first carried out on documents. The costs of the audit are borne by whoever requests it.

13. Data transfers outside the European Economic Area

Ospitalk's servers are in the European Union. Some sub-processors are established in the United States or may process data outside the European Economic Area. RGM transfers data outside the EEA only if a safeguard provided for in Chapter V of the GDPR is in place: the European Commission's adequacy decision for the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for certified providers, or the standard contractual clauses approved by the Commission (Decision (EU) 2021/914), with any supplementary measures. The safeguard used for each provider is shown on the sub-processors page.

14. Liability and final provisions

Each party is liable for damage caused by breaching the obligations the GDPR places on it, according to art. 82 GDPR. For anything not provided for here the Terms of service apply; in case of conflict on data protection this agreement prevails. Changes to this agreement follow the rules of article 16 of the Terms of service.
