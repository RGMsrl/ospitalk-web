A personal welcome, answers always available

WhatsApp assistant for B&Bs

Ospitalk is the WhatsApp assistant for your B&B guests: it answers questions about breakfast, arrivals, services and the stay with the information you provided. You keep looking after the personal welcome and step in from your WhatsApp only for requests that need a decision.

Activate on WhatsApp

It's set up in the chat with texts, photos and voice notes. You describe your B&B once and Ospitalk replies to each guest in their own language.

Times and breakfast room

Welcome

More time for people, fewer operational interruptions

Arrivals and when you're around

With the QR code at the property, or the link you send to guests who book directly with you, guests can ask about reception hours, how to let you know if they're running late and where to go.

Room amenities

Wi-Fi, air conditioning, housekeeping and amenities: the information stays easy to ask for and to find.

The rhythm of the house

Guests know when they can meet you and how the B&B works, without taking any warmth away from your welcome.

Local tips

Share the places you really know: how to get around, where to eat and what to see nearby.

A conversation that gets better

New questions become useful knowledge

When Ospitalk doesn't have an answer, you get the question on your WhatsApp. Your reply goes back to the guest and can be saved for similar requests. This way the assistant adapts to what makes your B&B special, without making up details.

You don't need to log in to a dashboard to reply. The host's working channel stays WhatsApp.

Privacy and pricing

Privacy for those who stay and those who host

At a B&B, guests scan the QR code in their room or in the breakfast room, without installing anything. Ospitalk doesn't show you their phone number: you see their WhatsApp profile name or “Guest no.” with a sequential number. If guests book directly with you, you can send them the link before they arrive.

Anyone running a property in Italy must also obtain and display the national identification code (CIN): to find out who needs one, where to display it and what penalties apply, read our CIN guide for short lets and accommodation (in Italian).
