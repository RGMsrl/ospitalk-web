Version 2026-10-30 · in force from 30 October 2026

You sign up for free from the chat, try the service for 30 days without a card and then, if you want to continue, choose a package and pay with Stripe. If you are a private individual you can withdraw within 14 days of payment and you only pay for the days used. The rules for hosts with a VAT number are in article 20.

2. Who these terms apply to and definitions

These terms govern the contract between RGM and anyone who uses Ospitalk to manage one or more accommodation properties ("host", "you").

  • Consumer host: an individual who uses Ospitalk for purposes outside any business or professional activity, for example someone who rents a home they own to tourists without a VAT number. The consumer host also benefits from the protections of the Italian Consumer Code (Legislative Decree 206/2005), which these terms do not limit in any way.
  • Professional host: anyone who uses Ospitalk as part of their business or professional activity, for example someone who gives a VAT number when signing up or paying. Article 20 also applies to the professional host.
  • Guest: anyone staying or intending to stay at the property who writes to Ospitalk's WhatsApp number.
  • Host content: the information about the property that the host gives Ospitalk (texts, photos, voice notes, times, rules, access codes).

3. What Ospitalk is

Ospitalk is a WhatsApp assistant for holiday homes, B&Bs and guest rooms. It works on a WhatsApp number shared between properties: the guest writes, for example by scanning the property's QR code, and the assistant answers automatically using the host content. If it doesn't know the answer, it forwards the question to the host, who answers from their own WhatsApp. The host sets up the service by chatting on WhatsApp and can use some extra features from a web page reached through a personal link.

4. Sign-up and conclusion of the contract

You sign up from your WhatsApp: write ACTIVATE OSPITALK (or ATTIVA OSPITALK) to +39 327 748 5039, receive the link to these terms, to the data processing agreement (which is an annex to them) and to the privacy notice, and reply ACCEPT. With the reply ACCEPT the contract is concluded. We record the version of the documents accepted, the date, the time and the phone number you replied from, and send you a confirmation on WhatsApp. You can ask us at any time for a copy of the documents in the version you accepted.

To sign up you must be of legal age and entitled to manage the property. For each property you must give its official identifier where your country requires one (in Italy the CIN, Codice Identificativo Nazionale).

5. Free trial

Every new sign-up includes a free 30-day trial. During the trial we don't ask for any payment card. At the end, if you don't choose a package, the automatic answers stop and we don't charge you anything: the trial never turns into a paid service by itself. Guests' questions keep reaching you for a period we tell you on WhatsApp.

6. Packages, prices and payment

To continue after the trial you can choose one of the available packages (for example Season, Annual or Monthly). Before payment we clearly show you the total price, the length of the package, taxes and any renewal. For consumer hosts prices include VAT.

Payment is made through Stripe, on a secure Stripe page: RGM neither sees nor stores your full card details. The paid contract is concluded when the payment is confirmed. We send you the receipt or invoice as required by tax law.

7. Duration, renewal and cancellation

Each package lasts for the period shown at the time of purchase.

The package renews only if, at the time of purchase, we clearly told you that automatic renewal applies. In that case we notify you on WhatsApp or by email before each charge: at least 7 days before for the Monthly package and at least 30 days before for the Season and Annual packages, reminding you of the price, the date and how to cancel. If we haven't sent you the notice, the package doesn't renew.

You can cancel at any time, at no cost, by writing to us on WhatsApp, at info@ospitalk.com or from the Stripe payment portal. Cancelling stops future renewals; the period already paid stays active until it expires, without prejudice to the right of withdrawal in article 8.

8. Consumer host's right of withdrawal (14 days)

If you are a consumer host you can withdraw from any paid contract within 14 days of its conclusion (that is, of payment), without giving reasons and without penalties. A clear statement is enough: write to us on WhatsApp, write to info@ospitalk.com or use the form at the bottom of this page (using the form is not compulsory). It is enough to send the communication before the 14 days run out.

Immediate start. When you pay we ask whether you want the paid service to start straight away, during the 14 days. If you expressly ask for this and then withdraw, we refund the price paid minus only the part proportional to the days of service already used up to your notice of withdrawal. If you don't ask for an immediate start, the paid service starts when the 14 days end. The right of withdrawal remains valid even if you asked for an immediate start.

We refund you within 14 days of your communication, using the same means of payment, at no cost to you.

9. The assistant and host content

Answers to guests are generated by an artificial intelligence system based on host content. For this reason:

  • the host undertakes to provide correct, complete and truthful content and to keep it up to date (for example times, house rules, access instructions, emergency contacts), correcting it as soon as it changes;
  • the host undertakes to check the summaries of the recorded information that Ospitalk sends during setup and to tell us straight away about any errors;
  • the assistant doesn't give information you haven't provided and, when it doesn't know the answer, passes the question to you; an automatic system can still make mistakes or misunderstand a question, and for matters that are important for the stay the guest can always ask to talk to you;
  • Ospitalk doesn't replace your presence in emergencies: it remains your job to be reachable and to step in when needed, and to give guests the emergency numbers;
  • Ospitalk tells guests that they are talking to an automatic assistant; the host must not present the assistant as a person.

Host content and conversations with guests are not used to train artificial intelligence models, either by RGM or by the providers that process the answers on our behalf. We use them only to provide the service to you.

Host content remains yours. You allow us to use it only to run Ospitalk for your property, for the duration of the contract.

10. Key box code

If you enter the code of a key box or of an entrance, the assistant sends it only to guests with a registered stay in progress, from check-in to check-out. The host undertakes to register stays correctly and to keep the code up to date from the chat with Ospitalk, changing it regularly and, if the access system allows it, after every guest.

11. Host obligations and prohibited uses

By using Ospitalk you undertake to:

  • give truthful data about yourself and the property and, where required, a valid official identifier for each property (in Italy the CIN), displaying it as required by law;
  • comply with the rules on short-term rentals and accommodation, including tax and public security rules;
  • inform guests about the use of Ospitalk and process their data according to the data processing agreement;
  • comply with the terms and policies of WhatsApp and Meta;
  • keep your personal access link and your phone safe, and tell us immediately if you think someone is using them in your place.

You may not use Ospitalk to:

  • ask guests for deposits, down payments, bank transfers or other payments outside the booking and payment channels already agreed, or send bank details and payment links;
  • send spam, advertising or messages to people who haven't written about a stay;

  • advertise properties that don't exist, aren't yours or lack the identifier required by law;
  • try to get around the service's protections or use it in a way that puts the shared WhatsApp number at risk.

In case of serious or repeated breaches of article 11 we may terminate the contract under art. 1456 of the Italian Civil Code, by written notice.
