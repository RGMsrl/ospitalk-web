WhatsApp assistant for guest houses

Ospitalk is the WhatsApp assistant for guest houses and room rentals (in Italy, “affittacamere”). Entrances, floors, rooms and shared spaces all raise different questions. Ospitalk uses your property's information to answer on WhatsApp and only brings you the requests it can't handle.

The specific problem

Not every room has the same instructions

One entrance may be at street level, another in the courtyard; one room has a balcony, another uses a shared bathroom. Ospitalk gathers the details in the chat and uses them in the right context.

Routes and entrances

Front door, entry phone, floor and keys can be described in words, photos and voice notes during setup; then send the link to guests before they arrive.

Amenities by room

The information can tell apart air conditioning, bathroom, view or other useful features of the stay.

Shared spaces

A shared kitchen, luggage storage and corridors have rules guests can ask about when they need them.

Day-to-day running

Fewer repeated messages, without losing control

Arrivals at different times

From the QR code displayed at the property, or the link if they booked directly with you, every guest can find the entry instructions without depending on you being available right away.

Linen and housekeeping

Explain how and when things are done at your property, avoiding different answers from one conversation to the next.

Guests living side by side

Quiet hours and use of shared spaces stay available in the chat, right when the question comes up.

Requests that need a decision

A room change or an exception lands on your WhatsApp. Reply there and Ospitalk delivers the answer to the guest.

Practical setup

Describe your property as you walk through it

You can set up Ospitalk by sending a photo of the front door, recording a voice note in front of a shared space or typing the rules for a room. You don't have to turn everything into a manual before you start.

For paid extras, such as transfers or late check-out, you only use the Services area. The link arrives on your WhatsApp when you send SERVIZI (Italian for “services”).

Privacy and pricing

Every guest's privacy is protected, even in shared spaces

With staggered arrivals in different rooms, the QR code at the entrance or in the room takes each guest to the property's chat. Ospitalk doesn't give you their phone number: you see their WhatsApp profile name or “Guest no.” with a sequential number. If guests book directly with you, you can send them the link before they arrive.

Anyone running a property in Italy must also obtain and display the national identification code (CIN): to find out who needs one, where to display it and what penalties apply, read our CIN guide for short lets and accommodation (in Italian).
