Privacy notice

For hosts and website visitors · arts. 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") · Version 2026-10-30

Are you a guest? This notice is about hosts and website visitors. For the messages you send to a property the data controller is the host: you'll find their notice on the property's page (an address like /p/property-name), whose link you receive in the first WhatsApp message. RGM processes that data on the host's behalf according to the data processing agreement.

1. Data controller

RGM S.r.l., Via Giuseppe Garibaldi 23, 95010 Santa Venerina (CT), Italy, VAT number and Catania Companies Register no. 05047840870, share capital € 10,000.00, paid-up € 2,500.00. Privacy contact: info@ospitalk.com. RGM has not appointed a data protection officer (DPO), as it is not required to.

2. What data we process

Hosts: sign-up and use of the service

  • the WhatsApp number and profile name you write ACTIVATE OSPITALK from;
  • proof of acceptance of the terms: version of the documents, date and time of the ACCEPT reply, number you sent it from;
  • property data: name, town, address, official identifier (in Italy the CIN), and the information you give during setup (texts, photos, voice notes and their transcripts);
  • the messages you exchange with Ospitalk, including the answers you give guests through the service;
  • technical data about access to the services web page (date and time, IP address, browser type).

Hosts: payments and invoicing

  • first and last name or company name, tax code or VAT number, address, email, recipient code or certified email (PEC);
  • package purchased, amounts, dates and status of payments. Card details are collected directly by Stripe: RGM only sees the last digits and the card network.

People who contact us

If you write to us by email or WhatsApp we process your address or number and the content of the message.

Visitors of the ospitalk.com website

  • technical data of requests (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser), recorded by the service that hosts the website for security reasons;
  • only if you consent, browsing data collected by Google Analytics (random browser identifier, pages viewed, visit duration, referrer, device and approximate geographic area). Details in the cookie policy.

The data requested for sign-up and payment is necessary: without it we can't provide Ospitalk. Consent to Analytics is optional and refusing it has no consequences for using the website. We don't send advertising and we don't sell your data. We don't take decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects on you.

Your content and messages are not used to train artificial intelligence models, either by RGM or by our providers.

4. Who we share the data with

The data is processed by RGM's authorised staff and by the providers listed on the sub-processors and providers page, appointed as processors: among others Meta (WhatsApp), Twilio (message delivery), Anthropic (automatic answers), Deepgram (voice note transcription), Contabo (servers in Germany), Hostinger (email), Stripe (payments), Google (Analytics, only with consent) and GitHub (website hosting). Meta and Stripe may also process some data as independent controllers, according to their own notices.

Invoicing data is shared with our accountant and with the Italian Revenue Agency through the Interchange System (Sistema di Interscambio), as required by law. We may share data with authorities only if the law requires it.

5. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Some providers are in the United States or may process data outside the EEA. In these cases the transfer is based on the adequacy decision for the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, for certified providers, or on the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. The safeguard used for each provider is shown on the sub-processors and providers page; you can ask for a copy at info@ospitalk.com.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and property data, messages with Ospitalk: for the duration of the contract and up to 90 days after it ends, so that you can ask for a copy.
  • Sign-ups not completed or trials not continued: deleted 90 days after the last message.
  • Invoices, accounting and payment data: 10 years, as required by Italian civil and tax law (art. 2220 of the Civil Code).
  • Proof of acceptance of the terms: 10 years from the end of the contract, the ordinary limitation period.
  • Contact messages: for as long as needed to reply and in any case no longer than 2 years, unless they become part of the contract.
  • Google Analytics: cookies last at most 13 months; browsing data is kept in Analytics for 14 months.

Guests' data follows the periods in the data processing agreement: it is deleted 90 days after check-out.

7. Your rights

You can ask at any time to access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict its processing, receive it in a readable format (portability) and object to processing based on legitimate interest (arts. 15-22 GDPR). You can withdraw consent to Analytics at any time from "Cookie preferences" at the bottom of every page, without affecting the lawfulness of the earlier processing.

Write to info@ospitalk.com or on WhatsApp at +39 327 748 5039: we reply within one month. Guests should first contact the host; if they write to us, we forward the request to the host.

If you believe the processing breaks the law you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, Piazza Venezia 11, 00187 Rome, or with the authority of the country where you live or work.

8. Changes

If we change this notice in a significant way, we notify hosts on WhatsApp or by email before the changes apply. The version in force is always on this page.
