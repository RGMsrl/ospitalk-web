Frequently asked questions

What to know before activating Ospitalk

Do I need to download an app or use a new number?

No. You use your own WhatsApp to activate Ospitalk, set up your property and answer forwarded questions. Guests write from their own WhatsApp. There's no app to download, for you or for them.

How do I activate Ospitalk?

Send ATTIVA OSPITALK (Italian for “activate Ospitalk”: it's the command the assistant recognises) to Ospitalk's WhatsApp number, +39 327 748 5039. Setup continues in the same chat with guided questions: you can send texts, photos and voice notes.

How do guests reach Ospitalk?

Guests who book through platforms find your property's QR code in the accommodation: scanning it is optional and opens WhatsApp. Platform rules may restrict links and contact details in their messages, which is why the channel for these guests is the QR code displayed at the property. If guests book directly with you, you can send them the property link before they arrive.

Can I see guests' phone numbers?

No. Ospitalk doesn't pass the guest's number on to you: in conversations and notifications you see their WhatsApp profile name or “Guest no.” followed by a sequential number. Guests are told about this when they first write.

What happens if the assistant doesn't know the answer?

You get the question on your WhatsApp and reply straight from there. Ospitalk delivers your answer to the guest and can use it as an automatic reply in the future, if you choose to save it.

What is the Services area for?

It's where you set up the paid extras you want to offer, such as transfers, breakfast or late check-out. To get the access link, send SERVIZI (Italian for “services”) in the Ospitalk WhatsApp chat.

Do I need a CIN?

If your property is in Italy, the CIN (Codice Identificativo Nazionale, the national identification code) must be displayed outside the building and shown in every listing, as required by Article 13-ter of Decree-Law 145/2023. During setup Ospitalk asks for your CIN: you can enter it now or skip that step and add it later, but you need a valid CIN to choose a plan. The obligations remain the owner's. You'll find the details in our CIN guide (in Italian).

Does Ospitalk send guest registrations to Alloggiati Web?

No. Reporting guests to the police (Questura) through Alloggiati Web remains up to you or your property management software. Ospitalk answers guests' questions before and during their stay and is not designed to collect identity documents.

Annual at €149: one-off payment, 12 months, no automatic renewal (if you pay during the free period, the 12 months start when it ends). Monthly at €19,90 a month: cancel whenever you like; if you subscribe during the free period, the first charge comes when it ends. Prices per property, VAT included. If you are a private individual you can withdraw within 14 days of payment: details in the terms of service.

Is there a limit on automatic replies?

Automatic replies are included in the price, with fair use: on a paid plan there's no number after which they stop. During the free period the assistant replies to up to 10 new guests a day per property. Only if a single guest sends dozens of messages in a short time do automatic replies to that guest pause for a while.
