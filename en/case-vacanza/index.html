Made for people who run a holiday home

WhatsApp auto-replies for your holiday rental guests

Ospitalk is the WhatsApp assistant for your holiday rental: from self check-in to recycling, it gives guests the instructions you've set up. If an unexpected request comes in, you get it on your WhatsApp and reply from there.

Before arrival

Self check-in explained, without chasing every guest

Codes, where the key box is, which floor, directions from the car park: they change from home to home. You explain them to Ospitalk once, with texts, photos or a voice note. Guests find them again by scanning the QR code in the home; if they book directly with you, you can send them the link before they arrive.

No confusion at the door

From the QR code in the home, or the link if they booked directly with you, guests can ask where the entrance is or how to open the key box and get the instructions you provided.

Visual directions

During setup you can send photos that help guests recognise the front door, the parking space or a detail of the entrance.

Exceptions under your control

A very early arrival or a request outside the rules is forwarded to your WhatsApp for you to decide.

During the stay

The house manual becomes a conversation

Wi-Fi and appliances

Passwords, boiler, air conditioning and small practical doubts are answered using your property's information.

Local and house rules

Quiet hours, rubbish and instructions for outdoor spaces are there whenever guests need them.

Tips that are truly yours

You can point out the handiest supermarket, the best place to park or the spots you personally recommend.

A smooth departure

Guests can find check-out time, what to do with the keys and the last instructions without digging through old messages.

When there's no answer

The question comes to you, it doesn't get lost

Ospitalk forwards the request to your WhatsApp. You reply in the same chat, Ospitalk delivers the message to the guest and, if you choose to save it, can reuse it automatically next time.

For day-to-day management, WhatsApp is all you need. The web-based Services area is for setting up paid extras, such as transfers or late check-out.

Privacy and pricing

The guest's number stays private, even with lots of bookings

Ospitalk doesn't give you the guest's phone number: you see their WhatsApp profile name or “Guest no.” with a sequential number, so chats with the people staying with you remain separate from your personal contacts. Guests who book through a platform find the QR code in the flat and use it if they want; if they book directly with you, you can send them the link before check-in.

Anyone running a property in Italy must also obtain and display the national identification code (CIN): to find out who needs one, where to display it and what penalties apply, read our CIN guide for short lets and accommodation (in Italian).
